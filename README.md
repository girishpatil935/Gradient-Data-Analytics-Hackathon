# Olist E-Commerce Business Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Brazilian Olist e-commerce marketplace using approximately 100,000 orders from September 2016 to October 2018.

The objective was to transform multiple raw e-commerce datasets into a validated, order-level analytical dataset and develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps identify:

- Overall marketplace performance
- Sales and revenue patterns
- Customer behavior and retention
- Delivery performance and delays
- Product category and freight patterns
- Customer reviews and satisfaction
- Business opportunities and recommended actions

## 🎯 Business Problem

Olist's data contains information across multiple business dimensions including orders, customers, products, sellers, payments, reviews and logistics.

The challenge was to bring these datasets together while maintaining data integrity and then answer important business questions such as:

1. How is Olist's marketplace performing over time?
2. Which product categories generate the most revenue and orders?
3. What are the characteristics of high-value customers?
4. What factors are associated with customer satisfaction and dissatisfaction?
5. How does delivery performance relate to customer experience?
6. Where are freight and operational costs creating pressure?
7. What actions should Olist prioritize?
